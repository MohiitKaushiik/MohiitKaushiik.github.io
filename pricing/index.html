Common Questions

How quickly do you deliver?

Essential and Premium plans are typically delivered within 24 hours. Grand plans get priority delivery — typically within 12 hours. Share your details in the morning, share your invitation by evening.

Can I see a demo before ordering?

Absolutely! Visit our live demos — we have engagement, wedding, birthday, and valentine examples you can explore right now.

What details do I need to provide?

Names of the couple/host, event date and time, venue address, any photos you want included, and your preferred language(s). Send it all via WhatsApp — no forms needed.

Can I make changes after delivery?

Yes. Essential and Premium plans include 2 rounds of revisions. Grand plan includes up to 5 rounds of revisions.

How do guests access the invitation?

You get a unique URL (like yournames.premiuminvitation.com) and a QR code. Share via WhatsApp, SMS, email, or print the QR on physical cards.

Do you support languages other than Hindi and English?

Hindi & English are built-in. Additional languages — Gujarati, Punjabi, Tamil, Telugu, Bengali, Marathi, Kannada, and more — are available on request. Just let us know your preferred language.
