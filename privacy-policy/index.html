1. Introduction

Welcome to Premium Invitation (premiuminvitation.com). We create premium 3D invitation websites for weddings, engagements, birthdays, and other celebrations. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal information when you use our services.

By using our website or engaging our services, you consent to the practices described in this policy in accordance with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDPA) 2023 and the Information Technology Act, 2000.

2. Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information:

a) Information You Provide via WhatsApp

  • Names of the couple, host, or family members
  • Phone numbers
  • Event details (date, time, type of ceremony)
  • Venue information
  • Photos and images for the invitation

b) Information Collected via RSVP

  • Guest names
  • Attendance status (attending / not attending)
  • Meal preferences (if applicable)

c) Automatically Collected Information

  • Device type and browser information
  • IP address
  • Pages visited and interaction data (standard web analytics)

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Create and deliver your custom invitation website
  • Communicate with you about your order and any revisions
  • Process RSVP responses and share attendance data with you
  • Improve our services and website performance
  • Comply with legal obligations

4. Sharing & Third Parties

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We may share data with the following third-party service providers solely to deliver our services:

  • Vercel — website hosting and deployment
  • WhatsApp (Meta) — client communication
  • Analytics providers — anonymous usage statistics

These providers are bound by their own privacy policies and only process data as necessary to provide their services.

5. Data Retention

We retain your invitation data (names, photos, event details) for up to 12 months after your event date. After this period, data is deleted upon request. RSVP data is retained for the same duration.

If you wish to have your data deleted sooner, you may contact us at any time using the details in Section 10 below.

6. Your Rights

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDPA) 2023, you have the right to:

  • Access — request a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • Correction — request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Erasure — request deletion of your personal data
  • Grievance redressal — raise concerns about how your data is processed

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the information in Section 10.

7. Children’s Data

Our services are not directed at children under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children without verifiable parental consent. If you believe we have inadvertently collected data from a child, please contact us and we will promptly delete it.

8. Security

We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your personal data against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. Our websites are served over HTTPS, and we use secure hosting infrastructure. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with a revised “Last updated” date. We encourage you to review this page periodically. Your continued use of our services after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
