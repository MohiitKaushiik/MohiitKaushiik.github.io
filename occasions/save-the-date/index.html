Common Questions

Frequently Asked

When should I send a save the date?

Save the dates are typically sent 3-6 months before the event. For destination weddings, 6-8 months is ideal. This gives guests enough time to plan travel, request leave, and arrange logistics.

Is the save the date different from the wedding invitation?

Yes! A save the date is a brief, early notification with just the date and location. The formal invitation comes later with full details — ceremony times, venue address, RSVP, dress code, etc.

Can guests add the date to their phone calendar from the website?

Yes! We include an add-to-calendar button that works with Google Calendar, Apple Calendar, and Outlook — one tap and the date is saved to their phone.
