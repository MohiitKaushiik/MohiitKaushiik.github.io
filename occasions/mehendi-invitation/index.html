Common Questions

Frequently Asked

Can the mehendi invitation include a dress code?

Yes! Many families specify colours or themes for the mehendi ceremony. Your invitation can prominently feature the dress code so guests come coordinated for those perfect photos.

Can I add background music to the mehendi invitation?

Absolutely. We can add a festive background track that plays when guests open the invitation — setting the mood instantly. Guests can toggle it on or off as they prefer.

Is the mehendi invitation separate from the wedding invitation?

It can be! Many families send separate invitations for each event (mehendi, sangeet, wedding, reception). We can create individual invitations or a multi-event invitation — whatever suits your celebration.
